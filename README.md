Duskline Best Advice — one sentence, the best advice the writer ever got: his father's words about speed, promises, and keeping them.

## The entry

> My father said, 'Son, nobody remembers how fast you finished — they remember whether you kept your word,' and twenty years on, I'm still slow, and I still keep it.

## Why this line

- **A real voice, a real moment:** a father speaking to his son, quoted directly — not a slogan.
- **Advice worth carrying:** speed is forgotten, a kept word is remembered — true well beyond the moment it was said.
- **Earned:** "twenty years on" shows the advice was lived, not just heard.
- **Tight:** 163 characters, one breath, every word earning its place.
- **It lands:** "I'm still slow, and I still keep it" — the son became the proof of the advice.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

The program validates the sentence first (one sentence, one line, UTF-8, 1–200 characters), then writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and nothing else, using only the Python 3.13 standard library.
